How it fits in 6 GB

Measured on this RTX 2060 (Turing, PCIe 3.0, 5.6 GiB usable).

fp16 everywhere

Turing has no fast bf16: measured 23 TFLOPS fp16 vs 3.6 TFLOPS bf16. Stock Z-Image overflows to black in fp16; the SVDQuant INT4 build stays stable and is 1.6× faster.

Layer streaming

Weights sit in pinned RAM; block i+1 copies over PCIe (13 GB/s) while block i computes. A 6B model peaks under 1 GB of VRAM for the transformer.

One shared text encoder

Both models use the bit-identical Qwen3-4B encoder. One streamed copy serves both: 0.6 s per prompt vs 12.6 s on CPU.
